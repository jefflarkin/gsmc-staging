# Cub Scout Information

Cub Scouts is a family-based, grade-specific program for boys and girls. Beginning in Kindergarten and continuing until the child is old enough to join Scouting BSA, cub scouts mix fun and adventure as they learn their place in the world around them.

[Learn More](https://easttnscouts.org/cubscouts/)
